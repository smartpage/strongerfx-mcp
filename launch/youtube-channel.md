Do not include “trending” against specific sounds without current evidence, or promise viral results. Use the existing copper artwork and dark/orange brand palette when adding artwork.

Create a separate business channel under the user's existing Google account. Do not rename their current channel. YouTube's documented route is the channel list → Create a new channel → name and handle → Create channel. [Official creation instructions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1646861?hl=en)

## 2. Clickable checkout route

YouTube Shorts URLs in descriptions and comments are **not clickable**. Pinning a comment does not change that. Channel profile links are clickable. Therefore, put the real Etsy product URL in the channel's **first profile link**, titled **Download the sounds — Etsy**. [YouTube link behaviour](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639)

Set this in Studio → Customization → Profile → Links. The first link is displayed prominently near Subscribe. Do not fill it with a guessed Etsy URL, the seller onboarding URL, or an unrelated demo catalogue. Leave the purchase link unset while the listing is unavailable. [Profile setup](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2657964?hl=en)

Destination variable: `ETSY_PRODUCT_URL` — **unresolved; no publishable listing URL confirmed**.

Use after the featured sounds are actually included in the live pack:

Pinned comment:

> Download them all 🎧 Tap strongerFX above, then “Download the sounds — Etsy” on our channel. Get the sounds from this Short in the Starter Pack — $1.

Short description:

> You know the sound. Now you've found it. 🎧
> Download them all → tap strongerFX, then the first link on our channel.
> Starter Pack: $1 on Etsy.
> #soundeffects #memesounds #videoediting

The requested phrase “link pinned” can be used in a longer video's CTA where the pinned external URL is clickable, provided advanced features are enabled. On Shorts, “details pinned” plus the explicit profile route is accurate and avoids sending buyers to an inert URL. Do not use a comment keyword on YouTube; **soundfx is Instagram only**.

Pinning comments itself requires advanced features, which can take up to 24 hours to appear after enablement. If pinning is unavailable, use “Download them all — first link on our channel” and omit “pinned” until verified. [YouTube pinning instructions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6000964?hl=en-GB)

## 4. Optional related-video route

Once advanced features are enabled, attach a public or unlisted channel video called **Download the sounds — StrongerFX pack preview** to each Short through its Related Video setting. This creates a clickable link beneath the channel handle. It must lead to a YouTube video on this channel, not directly to Etsy. [Related-video instructions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en)

The pack-preview video should demonstrate the exact included sounds, then link directly to the verified Etsy listing in its description and pinned comment. Avoid adding this extra step before a real preview video exists; the channel profile link is the immediate route.

Long-form pinned comment template:

> Download all the sounds featured here 🎧 Starter Pack — $1: [insert verified Etsy product URL]

Do not publish bracketed placeholders.

## 5. First three Shorts

1. **“You know this sound. What's it called?”** Play the sound immediately; reveal its actual name; show one editing use; close with the download CTA.
2. **“Same clip. Three sounds. Three different jokes.”** Repeat one original/cleared visual with three exact pack sounds; label each; close with the same CTA.
3. **“The sounds you keep hearing but can never find.”** Three named previews with recognisable moment labels: awkward silence, epic fail, plot twist; show their cards; close with the same CTA.

These are hook tests, not proven winners. Do not publish generic interface clicks under recognisable meme-sound claims. Only promise “them all” when every featured sound is included in the destination pack.

1. Confirm the new channel's name, handle and ownership under the intended existing account.
2. Save the description and branding; verify public rendering.
3. Add the real Etsy listing as the first profile link only after checkout and the actual downloadable product exist.
4. Upload the finished Short, add the accurate description and publish.
5. Post and pin the supplied CTA after publication, if advanced features permit it.
6. From the Shorts viewer, follow the stated route to the channel profile and Etsy. Confirm the pack matches every preview and the advertised price.
7. Keep Instagram's separate CTA exactly **“Comment soundfx to get the link.”** Its DM should identify the paid pack rather than imply that all sounds are free.

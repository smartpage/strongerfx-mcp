# StrongerFX — creator offer

This supersedes the generic Essentials / Original Transitions positioning in the earlier launch drafts. It is a product and copy specification, not a claim that the meme pack is already available.

## Positioning

Recognizable meme sound effects for short-form creators. Find the sound you keep hearing, preview it instantly, and download the file for your next edit.

The buyer already knows the sound but often cannot name it. Sell recognition and the shortcut from hearing to editing. Help a punchline land, communicate a reaction, or make a reveal feel familiar. Virality is the buyer's aspiration, not a guaranteed result.

## Landing copy — for the actual meme pack

Eyebrow: MEME SOUND EFFECTS FOR REELS, TIKTOK & SHORTS

Headline: You know the sound.\nNow you've found it.

Subhead: The reactions, punchlines and plot twists your audience recognises. Hear the sound. Grab the file. Drop it into your next edit.

Primary CTA: Play the sounds

Soundboard title: Oh. THAT sound.

Search placeholder: That awkward silence? The dramatic reveal?

Browse by moment: Awkward silence / Epic fail / Plot twist / Big reveal / Suspicious / Celebration.

Every card: actual recognisable name, searchable aliases, a short “use it when…” cue, play/pause, duration. One sound plays at a time. On mobile, previews appear immediately after the headline. Avoid making visitors navigate to a separate catalogue before hearing the product.

Offer title: Stop hunting. Start editing.

Offer copy: Download the sounds you just heard, together in one organised pack. Individual audio files, ready to import into your editor.

Purchase CTA when checkout and pack are ready: Get the pack — $1

Supporting line: One payment. Instant download.

Show the exact sound count and included formats only after the final pack is built. State the actual permitted uses. Do not say “use anywhere” without a licence supporting that promise. Do not display an invented crossed-out price or 70% discount.

Keep MCP and Apify in a small footer link labelled “For developers”. They are a separate distribution channel, not the creator's reason to buy.

## First Reel — search frustration

0–2s: Play the first recognisable sound immediately; overlay “Conheces este som. Mas sabes como se chama?” No logo intro.

2–9s: Three short previews, each labelled with its actual name and a concrete editing moment. Cut to a visual reaction or an example edit that makes the timing understandable.

9–12s: Show the soundboard playing the same sounds. Overlay “Encontraste. Agora é só descarregar.”

12–15s: “Comenta SONS para receberes o link do pack — $1.” Use this only once the delivery automation and checkout work. The link leads to a paid pack; do not promise a free download.

Caption: “Os efeitos sonoros que reconheces dos Reels, finalmente fáceis de encontrar. Ouve os exemplos e escolhe o que encaixa na tua próxima reação, falhanço ou reviravolta. Comenta SONS e recebe o link do pack de $1.”

## Validation and release

Test the search-frustration, name-reveal and before/after hooks using the same sounds and offer. Compare preview plays, visits to checkout and completed purchases per landing-page visit. Record sample sizes; likes alone do not validate a paid offer.

Before a clip enters the commercial pack: identify the exact recording, establish standalone redistribution permission, trim and check its audio, record provenance, and give it an honest name and editing cue. Establish trend claims with dated examples from multiple independent creators and the target audience; a single old compilation is insufficient.

The current 254-file CC0 catalogue is a working technical/demo library. It is not the requested recognisable meme product. Do not silently substitute it behind this copy. Exact meme previews, the final paid download and checkout remain incomplete.

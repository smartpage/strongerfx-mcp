## Positioning

For short-form creators who recognise a sound but cannot name or retrieve it, MemeFX turns recognition into an editable audio file. The immediate promise is finding this exact sound. Daily discovery supports repeat use. The community is secondary value, not the acquisition hook.

English-first is a deliberate user decision. Portuguese responses inform hypotheses; they do not validate English audience demand.

Message order: recognition → exact audible proof → easy retrieval → transparent paid access → recurring value.

Avoid leading with AI, 254 generic effects, technical audio specs, an abstract subscription pitch or developer tooling. Do not describe every sound as currently viral without dated supporting evidence.

## Reel system

Anchor hook: **Sounds you've been looking for but can never find.**

Create an original realistic AI presenter; do not impersonate the reference creator or pretend it is a customer. Keep the presenter consistent and subordinate to audio recognition. No speech over the effects. Apply platform synthetic-media disclosures as required when publishing.

First pilot storyboard (proposed timing, not an analysis of the original):

1. 0–2 seconds: hook on screen; immediate recognisable audio preview.
2. 2–10 seconds: three exact recordings, each paired with its matching meme image and searchable name. Leave enough time to recognise each; extend duration if needed.
3. 10–13 seconds: screen recording of those exact three homepage cards. Tap one and play the same file.
4. 13–17 seconds: “Recognise these? Comment SOUNDS and I’ll send you the collection.” Do not introduce pricing in the Reel CTA. Promise exact downloads only when those same files are available.

Caption draft:

Comment SOUNDS and I’ll send you the collection. Explore free and try your first download.

Release only once the advertised files, subscription and delivery are actually available. Preview access can be open without inventing a free downloadable pack.

Next episodes: named-sound requests; same frustration hook with another verified set; before/after editing examples. Keep the initial hook fixed while testing sound selections so the result is interpretable. A suggested daily cadence is an operational test, not an algorithm requirement.

## Main-page copy and layout

### First mobile screen

Eyebrow: MEME SOUNDS FOR YOUR NEXT EDIT

Headline: **Sounds you've been looking for but can never find.**

Subhead: **Recognise the meme. Play the sound. Find the exact one you came for.**

Immediately show the featured meme-image cards. No full-screen avatar hero, navigation detour, generic waveform placeholders presented as finished meme cards or autoplay audio.

Card fields: exact name; familiar aliases; actual matching meme image; play/pause; actual duration; concise “use it when” cue; download/member-access action. Preview and paid file must come from the same verified recording. One sound plays at a time. Exact asset unavailable means clearly unavailable, never a substitution.

### Collection and purchase

Section: **Heard it in our Reel? It's right here.**

Group the promoted recordings together, then expose searchable remaining cards on this same page. Search should accept phonetic aliases such as “faah”, subject to identifying that exact recording.

Offer headline: **Stop searching. Start editing.**

Offer body must describe available downloads accurately. Daily drops and community belong to the intended offer but must not be presented as delivered benefits until their delivery is implemented and verified.

Anonymous CTA: **Sign in to download**. Show **$3/month** in the pricing section, accessible from top and bottom navigation. After authentication, the subscription action opens account-bound checkout when payments are operational.

Billing disclosure: Monthly subscription. Explain cancellation and continued access accurately after implementation. No fake discounts, fabricated member totals or competitor comments disguised as reviews.

### Resolve the actual download question

1. Hear the exact sound.
2. Sign in to save your free selection; subscribe for additional available downloads.
3. Download and import it into your editor.

Verify file formats and editor compatibility before naming them in public copy. Explain music/platform restrictions and per-asset permissions accurately; standalone redistribution permission is a release dependency for paid files.

### Recurring value

Section: **Your next sound, already found.**

Show a real sample daily email with selected audio links, available downloadable effects and one use case. Clearly distinguish downloadable files from platform audio links. Display dates so additions are tangible. Never recycle unchanged files as “new”.

Community section: **Make something with it.** Sound requests, members' edits and AI workflows. Show actual access details when configured. Do not suggest the community already has activity or guaranteed personal coaching.

FAQ: Are these the same recordings as the Reel? How do I download? Can I import them into my editor? What arrives daily? What can I use each sound for? How do I cancel? Answers must match shipped behaviour.

## Validation and economics

Track per Reel: reach, unique keyword commenters, successfully delivered DMs, tracked landing visits, preview plays, checkout starts, paid subscriptions, refunds and later cancellations. Comments/reach tests acquisition interest; purchases/visits tests the offer. Do not combine gratitude comments with explicit access requests.

High comments with few visits: investigate DM delivery and relevance. High previews with few purchases: examine price expectations, trust, exact-match availability and offer clarity. Purchases followed by cancellations: investigate recurring usefulness; more posts alone cannot fix it.

Compare equal observation windows and similar sound sets; report raw denominators, not invented benchmarks. First review after several published pilots, rather than treating one successful post as proof. $3 × active paying members is gross revenue; acquisition, payment, support and content costs determine viability.
